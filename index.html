Before · During · After

One calm plan for every stage

Most of the stress after a storm comes from not knowing what to do next. We break it into three simple stages so you always know your next step.

  1. 01 · Prepare: get ready before it hits
    Photograph your home and belongings now. Secure shutters, gutters and loose items. Build a go-kit and a family plan. Keep key documents safe and backed up.
  2. 02 · During: stay safe and steady
    Follow local emergency guidance first. Shelter in the safest interior room. Stay off roofs and out of flood water. Keep phones charged and check on neighbors.
  3. 03 · Restore: rebuild with confidence
    Document damage clearly with dated photos. Prevent further damage with safe temporary fixes. Get written scopes from licensed pros. Keep every receipt and conversation in writing.

Join the PrepRestore contractor network

How does the network work?

Contractors set their own services, a daily service radius and a catastrophe radius. Your service area shows on the public contractor map, and homeowners in your ZIP codes are connected to you.

What does it cost?

No monthly fee. PrepRestore charges a 10% referral fee on the connected service, so you only pay when work comes through.

Where are you active today?

Roofing coverage in Hawaii, the Florida Panhandle, Alabama and Georgia, with the network growing by invitation.

How do I get in?

Ask for an invitation below, add your service area on the contractor map, or open the contractor roster & registration portal. Each contractor is reviewed once, then your service area goes live. Insurance carriers can also explore our carrier loss-prevention program.
